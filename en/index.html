Socializer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Version 2.0

Socializer is a mobile app that helps you meet people who are at the same venue as you. This policy explains which personal data is processed when you use the app, why and on what legal basis, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and what rights you have. It also serves as the privacy notice required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). If the Turkish and English versions differ, the Turkish version prevails.

Data controller: Fırat Öncü Şener, Seyyitömer Mahallesi, Hüseyin Kazım Sokak No: 53, Fatih / İstanbul, Türkiye. Contact: oncu.sener@gmail.com

In short

  • No ads, no selling of data, no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your location is only used while the app is open. The raw GPS position saved at check-in is deleted after 30 days.
  • Messages are stored encrypted and deleted 24 hours after the match ends.
  • Authorised staff can see chat content only while reviewing a reported chat, and every access is logged.
  • Socializer is for people aged 18 and over only.

Socializer does not use passwords. You sign in with an Ed25519 signing key that is created on your device and never leaves its secure storage; only the public key is sent to our server. We do not ask for your real name, email address or ID documents.

2. What other users can see

  • Your nickname, age, gender, bio, approved photos, verification badge, whether you are a Premium member, and the mode you chose (Flirt, Network, Chill).
  • Only people who have checked in at the same venue can see you in Discover, depending on the visibility you chose when checking in.
  • Your phone number, date of birth, exact location and device ID are never shown to other users.

3. Location data

Your location is only used while the app is open and in the foreground. There is no background location tracking; we do not know where you are when the app is closed.

  • Nearby venues: your location is sent to our server to find cafés, bars and restaurants around you. It is not saved for this purpose.
  • Check-in: you can only check in at a venue that is within 3 km of you. To confirm this, your GPS position at that moment is written to the check-in record. This raw position is deleted automatically after 30 days; only the venue, mode and time remain.
  • Notifications: your check-in locations from the last 30 days may be used to notify you when a venue near you gets busy or when there is an announcement for your area. You may also be notified of announcements for a venue where you checked in before. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
Your exact location is not sent to outside services. Venue lists and venue search go to Google Places and OpenStreetMap (Overpass, Nominatim) through our own server, not from your device. The server first rounds the coordinate to a grid of about 110 metres (the venue list uses an even coarser grid), so these services receive your general area, not the point where you are. When you search, the text you type is sent as well. Your identity, device ID and IP address are not part of these requests; they come from our server.

The map is drawn on your device by the Google Maps SDK. To load map images your device connects directly to Google, which receives your IP address and the map area you are viewing. Google's own privacy terms apply to that data.

Your IP address is used to check whether it matches the GPS position you send. This check runs entirely on our server with an offline database; your IP address is not sent to any outside service for this and is not stored in our database. If a serious mismatch is found, it is written to the server logs for security purposes.

4. Photos and content moderation

  • Every photo you upload is re-encoded on our server and all metadata, such as GPS and EXIF, is removed.
  • Photos are checked automatically with Google Cloud Vision: photos containing nudity or violence are rejected and never stored, and borderline ones are held for review by our staff before they go live.
  • The same check looks for a face in the photo, to prevent fake profiles. It only answers the question "is there a face in this photo?": no face recognition or face matching is performed, no biometric template is created, and the face data returned by Vision is not stored.
  • Your verification selfie is deleted from our server once the verification decision is made.
  • Nicknames and bios go through an automatic filter for profanity, insults and sexual content.

5. Messages and chat content

  • Message content is stored in our database encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Chats are text only; photos, videos and files cannot be sent.
  • When a match ends (the chat time runs out, the match is removed, one person blocks the other or deletes their account), the messages in that chat are permanently deleted from our server 24 hours later.
  • Exception: if there is an open report between the two people, the chat is kept until the review is finished and deleted once it is closed. In every case it is deleted no later than 180 days after the match ended.
  • Staff access: authorised staff in our admin panel can see the content of a chat only if there is a report between its participants, and only to review that report. Every view is logged together with who viewed it. The content of chats that have not been reported cannot be read; only technical details (participants, date, number of messages) are visible.
  • New-message notifications contain the sender's nickname and the first 40 characters of the message and are delivered through the notification services listed below. These previews in your in-app notification list are deleted together with the messages.

6. Encryption and security

Your nickname, phone number and message content are encrypted in our database with AES-256-GCM, so someone with direct database access cannot read them. For fields that need to be searchable or unique (nickname, phone), we also keep an irreversible cryptographic hash (blind index). All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS.

When the app is first opened it generates a random device ID on your device. It is not an advertising ID, it is not derived from your hardware and it is not shared with anyone. Its only purpose is to stop someone you have a block with from deleting their account and showing up again from the same device with a new one. Uninstalling the app removes the copy on your device.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Menu › Security & Privacy › Delete Account Permanently. Your profile, photos, the messages you sent, your eye contacts, notifications, blocks and all check-in locations are deleted immediately. Your phone number and device ID are kept for 6 more months to prevent abuse. The account deletion page explains in detail what is deleted and what is kept, and for how long.

10. Blocking and reporting

You can block or report anyone who bothers you, right in the app. Blocks and reports work both ways: you will not see each other in Discover and neither of you can send eye contacts or messages. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team, and you will not see the person you reported at any venue again. The rules and sanctions are set out in our Terms of Use.

11. Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it;
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose;
  • know the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom it is transferred;
  • ask for it to be corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate;
  • ask for it to be deleted or destroyed under the conditions of KVKK Article 7;
  • ask for corrections and deletions to be passed on to the third parties who received it;
  • object to a decision against you that results solely from automated processing;
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

If you are in the European Union, you also have the GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability.

To use these rights, write to oncu.sener@gmail.com. We will resolve your request within 30 days at the latest, and we may ask for extra information to confirm that the account is yours. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK) or, in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

12. Age limit

Socializer is only for users aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and people under 18 cannot register. If we find an account that belongs to someone under 18, we close it. See also our Child Safety Standards.
