Socializer — Account and Data Deletion

Developer: Fırat Öncü Şener · App: Socializer (Android: com.socializer.app · iOS: com.oncusener.socializer)
Last updated: 22 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Socializer account and the data linked to it, what is deleted right away and what is kept for a while longer. There are two ways: delete it yourself in the app, or send us an email.

1. Delete in the app

  1. Open Socializer and sign in.
  2. On the map screen, tap your profile photo in the top-left corner to open the menu.
  3. Choose Security & Privacy.
  4. Under Account, tap Delete Account Permanently.
  5. Read the warning and confirm with Delete My Account.

Deletion happens immediately and you are signed out. It cannot be undone, and the account cannot be signed into again.

2. Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app (you changed phones, uninstalled the app or your account was suspended), you can send your request by email:

oncu.sener@gmail.com

Use the subject line Account Deletion Request and include your account's nickname and phone number. We may ask for extra information to confirm that the account is yours. We will complete the request within 30 days and confirm it to you in writing. Your account is deleted to the same extent as an in-app deletion (sections 4 and 5 below).

3. Deleting data without deleting your account

  • You can remove your profile photos and bio on the Edit Profile screen. A photo you remove disappears from your profile right away and is not shown to anyone in the app; its file on our server may not be deleted right away. If you want it deleted, just write to the address above.
  • You can delete notifications one by one or all at once from the notification list.
  • You don't need to do anything for chats: messages are deleted automatically 24 hours after the match ends. If there is an open report between you, the chat is kept until the review is finished, at most 180 days.
  • The GPS position saved at check-in is deleted automatically after 30 days.

For a broader request, just write to the address above.

5. Data kept for a while longer

DataHow long and why
Your phone number (encrypted), the lookup hash of your phone number and your device ID 6 months. To make it harder for someone who broke the rules to delete their account and start over straight away with the same number, and to stop venue blocks tied to the device ID from being bypassed with a new account. During this time the same number can't be verified on a new account.
Venue blocks tied to your device ID 6 months. Deleted together with the device ID. If another account uses the same device, they are kept because they protect that account too.
Report and moderation records (including staff notes about the account) Kept for user safety, linked to the anonymised account. Once the phone number and device ID are deleted after 6 months, these records can no longer be linked to you.
Messages the other person sent you Because the match ends when you delete your account, they are deleted within 24 hours. If there is an open report between you, they are kept until the review is finished, at most 180 days after the match ended, and deleted once it is closed.
Check-in and match records Kept without location or message content (venue, mode and time only), linked to the anonymised account and used for venue statistics.
Your push token (the address used to send notifications to your device) While the app stays on your device, general announcements may keep reaching it. When you uninstall the app the token becomes invalid and is deleted from our server at the next send. If you want it deleted right away, write to the address above.
Files of photos you removed from your profile earlier, and of a verification photo still waiting for review No longer linked to your account and not shown to anyone in the app, but they may not be deleted from our server right away. If you want them deleted, just write to the address above.
SMS sending and verification records Kept with a hash of your phone number, not linked to your account, to limit SMS abuse.
Other fields of the account record (your settings, last online time, Premium and founding member details, your invite code and who invited you, daily message and photo moderation counters) Kept, linked to the anonymised account; they contain no name, phone number or photo.
Purchase and subscription records (if any) Kept, linked to the anonymised account, for the period required by tax and accounting law.

After 6 months: 180 days after your account is deleted, the phone number, phone hash and device ID on the account record are deleted automatically by a job that runs every night; you don't need to ask. From then on, the anonymised account record can't be matched to your phone number or device. If you want this data deleted before then, write to the address above; we will assess your request under the KVKK and let you know the outcome.

In addition, where a legal obligation requires it (for example an official request as part of a criminal investigation), the relevant records may be kept for the period set by law. In that case we will inform you if we are legally allowed to.

6. Questions

For any question about deletion, write to oncu.sener@gmail.com. For the full picture of how your data is processed, see our privacy policy.
