Socializer Child Safety Standards

Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) · Last updated: 22 October 2026

Socializer is for adults aged 18 and over only. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). When such content or behaviour is found, the account is closed immediately and permanently, and the matter is reported to the authorities.

1. What is prohibited

  • Creating, sharing, requesting, storing or pointing others to child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual communication with a minor, or gaining a minor's trust in order to abuse them (grooming).
  • Any content or language that sexualises minors.
  • Sextortion, child trafficking, or any attempt to arrange a meeting with a minor.
  • A person under 18 using the app, or helping someone under 18 to use it.

2. Age limit

  • We ask for the date of birth at sign-up; people under 18 cannot create an account.
  • Accounts found or suspected to belong to someone under 18 are reviewed and closed.
  • The 18+ requirement is stated clearly in our Terms of Use.

3. Preventive measures

  • Photo moderation: every uploaded photo is checked automatically with Google Cloud Vision. Photos containing nudity are rejected and never stored; borderline ones are held for review by our staff before they go live.
  • Text filter: nicknames and bios are filtered automatically for sexual and inappropriate language.
  • Limited chat: photos, videos and files cannot be sent in chat; only two matched people can talk, by text, for a few minutes.
  • Location privacy: users can't see each other's exact location; only people checked in at the same venue can see each other.

4. Reporting in the app

If you think a user is putting a child's safety at risk:

  1. Tap the menu icon (⋯) on their profile card in Discover or at the top of the chat screen and choose Report.
  2. Choose the Child safety category and send.

Child safety reports go to a priority queue and our staff are alerted immediately. You will not run into the person you reported anywhere in the app again. If you can't access the app, send your report to oncu.sener@gmail.com with the subject "Child Safety".

5. Review and enforcement

  • Child safety reports are handled before all other reports; we aim to review them within 24 hours.
  • When a violation is confirmed, the content is removed and the account is permanently closed without warning.
  • The owner of a closed account is prevented from signing up again with the same phone number or device.
  • The relevant records (including the chat concerned) are preserved for the review and for reporting to the authorities.

6. Reporting to the authorities

When we find CSAM or receive information that a child is in danger, we report it to the competent authorities:

  • Türkiye: İhbar Web, the cybercrime units of the Turkish National Police (Emniyet Genel Müdürlüğü) and public prosecutors' offices.
  • International: where appropriate, the CyberTipline of the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

We respond to requests from law enforcement and judicial authorities within the legal framework. If you believe a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number right away (112 in Türkiye and the EU). You can also report content directly at ihbarweb.org.tr or the CyberTipline.

7. Legal compliance

We comply with the Turkish Criminal Code (in particular Article 103 on the sexual abuse of children and Article 226 on obscenity), Law No. 5651 and other applicable legislation. We also follow Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and the Apple App Store Review Guidelines.
