4. How the app works

  • Check-in: when you allow location access, you see the venues around you. You can only check in at a venue that is within 3 km of you. When checking in you choose a mode (Flirt, Network, Chill) and a duration; the check-in closes by itself when the time is up.
  • Visibility: in Discover you only see people who have checked in at the same venue. When checking in you choose who you want to see, and whoever you pick can see you too. With Premium's Incognito option you see the people at the venue without appearing in Discover; only the person you send an eye contact to can see you.
  • Eye contact: you send an eye contact to someone you like. If they accept within a short time, you match.
  • Short chat: a match opens a chat that lasts a few minutes. The goal is not long texting but saying hello in person. When the time is up the chat closes and the messages are deleted as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • We may change features, durations and limits to improve the service.

5. Rules of conduct and zero tolerance

The following is strictly prohibited on Socializer:

  • Harassment: bullying, threats, stalking, persistent unwanted messages, or bothering someone inside or outside the app.
  • Hate speech: demeaning or discriminating against people because of race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar characteristics.
  • Sexual content: nudity, obscene photos or text, unsolicited sexual messages, offering or requesting sexual services.
  • Child abuse: any sexual content involving minors, abuse of minors, or any attempt at it. The account is closed immediately and permanently, and the matter is reported to the authorities.
  • Fake accounts: fake profiles, impersonating others, misleading age or identity, coming back with a new account after one was closed.
  • Other people's photos: photos that are not yours or that you have no right to share, and images showing other people without their consent.
  • Spam and commercial use: advertising, promotion, automated messages, bots, bulk accounts.
  • Scams: asking for money, gifts or personal information, investment or crypto offers, romance scams.
  • Violence: threatening or encouraging violence, promoting self-harm, illegal activities.
  • Privacy violations: sharing someone else's personal details, location or chat without their consent.
  • Abusing the system: fake location (fake GPS, VPN), exploiting security flaws, reverse engineering the app, scraping data, trying to get around blocks or sanctions.

Photos, nicknames and bios pass through automatic filters. Filters are not perfect; if you see something that breaks the rules, please report it.

6. Reporting and blocking

  • Tap the menu icon on a profile card in Discover (⋯), at the top of the chat screen or in the Who Looked At You list (⋮). On an incoming eye contact, the Block or report link opens the same options.
  • Report: pick a category (Inappropriate behaviour, Harassment or threats, Fake profile, Child safety, Something else). Your report goes to our moderation team. You will not see the person you reported at any venue again, and they will not see you.
  • Block: if you want to keep away from someone without reporting them, you can block them. Blocking works both ways and at every venue: you won't see each other in Discover or in Who Looked At You, neither of you can send eye contacts or messages, and any match between you ends. You can unblock later from the blocked list on the Security & Privacy screen, but a separation that comes from a report cannot be lifted.
  • If you can't access the app, you can send your report to oncu.sener@gmail.com.
  • If someone's life is in danger, call your local emergency number first (112 in Türkiye and the EU).

7. Moderation and sanctions

  • We review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks the rules and apply the sanctions below to the account that posted it. Child safety reports are handled first.
  • While reviewing a report, authorised staff can see the content of chats between the two people the report concerns. This access is logged. Chats between people with no report between them are not read.
  • Content that breaks the rules is removed. Depending on how serious the violation is, we issue a warning, suspend the account for a period or close it permanently. Serious violations (such as child abuse or real threats) get no warning.
  • The owner of a closed account may be prevented from signing up again with the same phone number or device.
  • An account that receives reports from many different people in a short time may be suspended automatically until the review is complete.
  • Unlawful content and situations that suggest a crime may be reported to the competent authorities.
  • To appeal a decision, write to oncu.sener@gmail.com.
