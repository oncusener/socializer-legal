4. How the app works

  • Check-in: when you allow location access, you see the venues around you. You can only check in at a venue that is within 3 km of you. When checking in you choose a mode (Flirt, Network, Chill) and a duration; the check-in closes by itself when the time is up.
  • Visibility: in Discover you only see people who have checked in at the same venue. When checking in you choose who you want to see, and whoever you pick can see you too.
  • Eye contact: you send an eye contact to someone you like. If they accept within a short time, you match.
  • Short chat: a match opens a chat that lasts a few minutes. The goal is not long texting but saying hello in person. When the time is up the chat closes and the messages are deleted as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • We may change features, durations and limits to improve the service.

Photos, nicknames and bios pass through automatic filters. Filters are not perfect; if you see something that breaks the rules, please report it.

6. Reporting and blocking

  • Tap the menu icon (⋯) on a profile card in Discover or at the top of the chat screen. The same options are available in the Who Looked At You list.
  • Report: pick a category (Inappropriate behaviour, Harassment or threats, Fake profile, Child safety, Something else). Your report goes to our moderation team. You will not see the person you reported at any venue again, and they will not see you.
  • Block: if you want to keep away from someone without reporting them, you can block them. Blocking works both ways and at every venue: you won't see each other in Discover or in Who Looked At You, neither of you can send eye contacts or messages, and any match between you ends. You can unblock later from the blocked list on the Security & Privacy screen, but a separation that comes from a report cannot be lifted.
  • If you can't access the app, you can send your report to oncu.sener@gmail.com.
  • If someone's life is in danger, call your local emergency number first (112 in Türkiye and the EU).

7. Moderation and sanctions

  • We aim to review reports within 24 hours. Child safety reports are handled first.
  • While reviewing a report, authorised staff can see the content of the reported chat. This access is logged. Chats that have not been reported are not read.
  • Content that breaks the rules is removed. Depending on how serious the violation is, we issue a warning, suspend the account for a period or close it permanently. Serious violations (such as child abuse or real threats) get no warning.
  • The owner of a closed account may be prevented from signing up again with the same phone number or device.
  • An account that receives reports from many different people in a short time may be suspended automatically until the review is complete.
  • Unlawful content and situations that suggest a crime may be reported to the competent authorities.
  • To appeal a decision, write to oncu.sener@gmail.com.
